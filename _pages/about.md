I am a Principal Researcher at **M365 Research**. My recent research focuses on
**reliable and self-improving AI agents**, **AI efficiency**, and
**privacy-preserving AI**.

My research has contributed to Microsoft products including Microsoft 365, Bing,
and Azure. I have authored nearly 50 peer-reviewed publications across leading
AI/ML conferences and physics journals.

I serve as an area chair for ACL Rolling Review and a reviewer for leading AI/ML
conferences, including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, and AAAI. Before transitioning to AI/ML,
I was a physicist with a Ph.D. in High Energy Physics from the University of Florida.

*The photo on this page was taken by my daughter when she was three years old. I'm wearing her hat, too! :)*

## Research interests

- **Reliable and self-improving agents:** understanding agent failures and turning
  experience into durable improvements.
- **AI efficiency:** making AI systems more efficient in inference and deployment.
- **Privacy-preserving AI:** understanding and addressing privacy challenges in
  AI systems.
